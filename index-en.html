What it does

Festivals, friends, trips, memories

No. 1

Festivals

Explore swing festivals across Europe, mark where you want to go and keep track of where you've already danced.

No. 2

Community

Find the friends from the scene and use the announcements board among those heading out: passes, car rides, spare beds, competition partners.

No. 3

Group trips

Organise the trip with chat, polls, checklists and shared logistics. Expenses split themselves, no spreadsheets.

No. 4

Your diary

Years of dancing, countries visited, milestones reached: the summary of your journey as a dancer.
